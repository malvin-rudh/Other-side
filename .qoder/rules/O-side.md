# O-Side: core product contract

- Preserve the proposed Mirae feature positioning. Historical replay, fictional sample data and provider market data must be labelled according to their actual mode. Proposed company-health screening and market-wide scanning must not be presented as implemented.
- Cards separate Observed, Interpretation and Unknown. Evidence strength (Strong, Moderate, Weak, Not enough data) describes support for one statement with a one-line reason, never a probability or confidence percentage.
- Never produce trade instructions, price targets, stock recommendations or rankings, individual-investor identification, matched-counterparty claims, inferred customer holdings or profit, or manipulation allegations. Evaluator-entered owned quantities may support the explicitly disclosed fixed-quantity portfolio model; they do not establish broker customers' positions or actual account returns.
- Broker profiles do not establish customer identity. Use "institutional brokers", "retail app brokers", "other / mixed brokers" and "in the visible sample". Apply exactly the same methodology to YP (Mirae Asset Sekuritas) as every other broker.
- Every factual number on screen opens relevant evidence for that symbol, statement and consumed window. Do not substitute unrelated facts or full-history values.
- Both locales (en, zh-CN) express the same accepted facts, predicates, caveats and claims. Missing data stays unknown, never silently zero or invented.
- Preserve provenance. Compute results from validated inputs rather than hard-coding expected numbers. Never manufacture captures, citations, sources, passing tests, cloud receipts or Qoder logs. Explicitly labelled fictional fixtures remain allowed within their assigned scope.
- No future data or claims may leak into a historical view through the UI, DOM, tooltips, evidence or AI context. Live quotes remain a separate surface from replay data and completed daily closes.
- Simulated order sheets send no trading request. Keep the historical header badge honest: "Proposed Mirae feature · Historical replay · data through {date}"; other modes keep their own accurate disclosure.
- Credentials belong only in server or local generation environments. Never put secrets in VITE_* variables, public JSON, source control, browser bundles or transcripts. The evaluator's demo token stays in memory and travels in the Authorization header. CORS is not authentication. Validate HTTP input at runtime.
- Read the relevant subsystem rules before editing its code. These are project instructions, not a substitute for runtime validation and access controls.
